# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

desktop (native UI drawn with MyGo in Go; macOS first, Windows and Linux possible). Not a web page: no browser, no CSS; Arabic text is shaped by the OS text engine (Core Text on macOS).

## Users

Muslims, mainly Indonesian, who read the Quran daily on their laptop and want to keep a steady habit (tilawah). Tajweed help is a secondary aid for reading correctly, not the main job. Interface in Bahasa Indonesia or English.

## Product Purpose

Make daily Quran reading easy to start and easy to keep: open the app and continue exactly where you stopped, in a calm view, with tajweed colors, translation and recitation there when wanted. Success: the user reads most days and reaches their daily goal.

## Positioning

A free, offline-first desktop Quran reader that shows the pages of the Madinah Mushaf with tajweed colors you can hover to learn, follows a reciter verse by verse, and counts verses actually read (time on screen in reading order), not pages opened.

## Operating Context

- Opened at a regular moment of the day (after prayer, morning, before sleep) for a session of minutes.
- Two ways to read: verse by verse with translation, or the Mushaf page as printed.
- Listening along to a reciter, sometimes repeating a verse.
- Occasional shuffle for review or memorization tests.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Full Quran text (Tanzil Uthmani, with open tajweed annotations), the Madinah Mushaf's 604 pages (each starting where the print does; lines broken by Quranku, 15 a page), 17 tajweed rules with Indonesian and English explanations.
- Translations: Pickthall (public domain) built in; Kemenag RI and 125 more downloadable from quran.com, Kemenag fetched automatically in Indonesian.
- 12 reciters from quran.com, verse-by-verse audio, downloadable for offline use.
- Reading statistics, daily goal, streak, progress by juz; shuffle with memorization test.
- Fonts limited to those Core Text renders correctly (Amiri Quran, Scheherazade New); the KFGQPC and Digital Khatt fonts do not render their vowel marks under Core Text.
- Settings and statistics live in the app's user data folder.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Quranku (قرآنكو).
- Tajweed hover highlights the letters alone, never boxes; emphasis by fading what is around (user preference, confirmed twice).
- Rule colors follow quran.com's tajweed palette.

## Evidence on Hand

No users, testimonials, or metrics yet. Text and pages are credited to Tanzil, tajweed to quran-tajweed, downloaded translations and audio to quran.com; fonts to the Amiri Project and SIL. Do not fabricate reviews or usage numbers.

## Product Principles

1. Continue reading comes first: the place you stopped is one click, or zero, away.
2. The Quran text is the hero; chrome recedes while reading.
3. Learning aids (tajweed, translation, audio) are present but optional, and never compete with the text.
4. Respect the reader's comfort: theme, font, size and spacing are theirs to set, and remembered.
5. Honest progress: count what was read, never inflate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Long reading sessions: comfortable contrast in light and dark, adjustable text size, keyboard navigation for page turning. Bilingual (Indonesian, English).
